## Rules

1. Do not noindex these pages solely because they have low clicks.
2. Preserve their canonical URLs.
3. Avoid unsupported guarantees such as exact compression quality or file-size outcomes.
4. Improve content around the exact user task represented by each URL.
5. Link each page to its parent tool and closely related variants.
6. Re-evaluate performance after Google has had time to recrawl the updated pages.

## Next implementation
