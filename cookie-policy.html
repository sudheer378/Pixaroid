1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit them. They help websites remember information about your visit, making it easier to navigate and use the site effectively.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them
  • First-party cookies: Set by the website you're visiting
  • Third-party cookies: Set by external services embedded in the website

2.1 Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly:

  • Session Management: Enables tools to work correctly during your browsing session
  • Security: Helps protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks
  • Load Balancing: Ensures stable performance during high traffic

Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for core functionality.

2.2 Preference Cookies (Optional)

These cookies remember your choices to improve your experience:

  • Theme Preference: Remembers if you prefer dark or light mode
  • Language Settings: Stores your preferred language (if applicable)
  • UI Preferences: Remembers collapsed/expanded sections

2.3 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We may use privacy-focused analytics to understand how users interact with our site:

  • Page Views: Which pages are most visited
  • Traffic Sources: How users find our website
  • User Behavior: General patterns (not individual tracking)

Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. We do not track individuals across websites.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Pixaroid may include content from third-party services that could set their own cookies:

  • Vercel (Hosting): May set cookies for security and performance monitoring
  • Google Fonts: May collect anonymous data about font loading
  • Analytics Services: If enabled, may set cookies for usage statistics

These third parties have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them for more information.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You have full control over cookies through your browser settings. Here's how to manage them:

4.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View all cookies stored on your device
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites

4.2 Instructions by Browser

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
  • Mobile Browsers: Check your browser's settings menu for privacy options

4.3 Opt-Out Tools

You can also use these resources to manage cookies across websites:

  • About Ads Choices
  • Your Online Choices
  • Network Advertising Initiative

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies, some features of Pixaroid may not work properly:

  • Theme preferences won't be saved
  • Some tools may require re-authentication on each page load
  • Analytics won't function (which helps us improve the site)
  • Overall user experience may be reduced

However, core image and PDF processing tools will continue to work even with cookies disabled, as they run entirely in your browser.
