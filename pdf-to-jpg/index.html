FAQs

What DPI should I use?

For screen viewing, 72-144 DPI is fine. For printing, use 216+ DPI.

Are files uploaded?

No, everything happens in your browser.

Can I extract specific pages?

Yes, uncheck "All Pages" and select individual pages.
