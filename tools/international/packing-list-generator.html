Tips for Smart Packing

  • Roll clothes instead of folding to save space and reduce wrinkles
  • Use packing cubes to organize items by category or outfit
  • Pack heavier items at the bottom of your luggage
  • Keep essentials and valuables in your carry-on bag
  • Check weather forecasts before finalizing your packing list
  • Bring versatile clothing that can be mixed and matched
  • Don't forget chargers and adapters for your destination
  • Pack a small first-aid kit with basic medications
