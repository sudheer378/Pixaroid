FAQs

Is the conversion accurate?

We use advanced text extraction that preserves most formatting and layout. Complex layouts may have minor differences.

Are files uploaded to a server?

No! All processing happens entirely in your browser for maximum privacy.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs require OCR which is not yet supported. Text-based PDFs work best.
