Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to split PDFs here? ▼

Yes. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Can I extract non-consecutive pages? ▼

Absolutely. Use the "Extract Pages" mode to individually select any pages you need.

Is there a file size limit? ▼

Since processing is local, the limit depends on your device's RAM, typically handling files up to 200MB+ smoothly.
