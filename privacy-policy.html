Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

Key Promise: Pixaroid processes all images and PDFs directly in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Your files never leave your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pixaroid ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website at https://pixaroid.vercel.app and our image and PDF processing tools.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your data. Our tools are designed to process files locally in your browser, meaning your files are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No File Uploads: All image and PDF processing happens entirely in your browser using client-side technologies (JavaScript, WebAssembly, Canvas API). Your files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • No Account Required: You can use all our tools without creating an account or providing personal information.
  • No Payment Information: All tools are completely free. We do not collect any payment or billing information.

2.2 Information We May Collect Automatically

  • Browser Information: Standard browser analytics may collect anonymous information such as browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to understand how users interact with our tools (e.g., which tools are most popular).
  • Cookies: We use minimal cookies for essential functionality like remembering user preferences. See our Cookie Policy for details.

3. How We Use Information

Any anonymous information we collect is used solely for:

  • Improving our tools and user experience
  • Understanding usage patterns to develop new features
  • Ensuring the security and integrity of our website
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect anonymous data:

  • Analytics: We may use privacy-focused analytics services to understand website traffic. These services do not track individual users across websites.
  • Hosting: Our website is hosted on Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel may collect server logs for security and operational purposes.
  • CDN: Static assets may be served through a Content Delivery Network for performance.

These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

5. Data Security

Since we don't store your files or personal data, there is no risk of data breaches affecting your uploaded content. Our website uses HTTPS encryption to protect data in transit between your browser and our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our tools are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since our processing happens locally in the browser, children can safely use our tools without any data collection concerns.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request information about what data we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion: Since we don't store your files, there is nothing to delete. Browser cookies can be cleared through your browser settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through a notice on our website.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@pixaroid.vercel.app
  • Contact Form: https://pixaroid.vercel.app/contact

10. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for processing any personal data is:

  • Legitimate Interests: For analytics and website improvement
  • Consent: For optional cookies (managed through cookie consent)

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA. Since we do not sell personal information and do not store user files, your privacy is inherently protected when using Pixaroid.
